# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy; never post another person's Vault content, credentials,
  private communications, or identifying diagnostics.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces or when publicly
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
private contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, reject
participation, or temporarily/permanently ban a contributor when behavior is
inappropriate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with
fairness to everyone involved.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
